What we do

Research

Our group carries out experimental and computational research in the thermal sciences, covering heat transfer, fire science, combustion, and bioenergy.

Our interests span two broad themes:

  • Developing more efficient, safe, and durable energy storage systems
  • Understanding the fundamentals of ignition and fire spread to prevent damage to people, property, and the environment from unwanted fires — including wildfires and fires from electrification

Current projects focus on wildfire dynamics, battery fires, thermal management of lithium-ion batteries, and ignition research.
